## Description

A party spectrum board game for 2–6 players. Hinters steer everyone toward a hidden goal on a two-axis word grid with short clues, and the guesser hops square by square to find it. Play over Steam or LAN in English, Chinese, Japanese, or Korean.

## About

Hint Hop is a multiplayer party game built around a spectrum board. One player is the guesser. Everyone else is a hinter.

Axes are word pairs, such as cold to hot. A goal cell is hidden from the guesser. Hinters take turns giving short clues that point toward that cell. After each hint, the guesser picks one square and hops there. Finding the goal in fewer guesses wins the round.

Play with 2–6 people online in a Steam lobby or on a local network. Roles rotate so everyone can take a turn as guesser. Optional phase timers keep the pace up, and the same lobby can start a rematch. The interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. Word packs ship in English and Chinese.

Hint Hop comes to Steam for Windows, macOS, and Linux on November 16, 2026, at 1:00 PM EST (10:00 AM PST). The price is $2.99 USD. The game is made in Godot 4.6 by William Wang (Xuyang) and published by Xylean.
